# Changelog

## [0.3.0] - In Development (WIP)

### Added
- **Starting Hero Selection:** Choose your starting squad leader before entering the dungeon.
- **Expanded Enemy Roster:**
  - *Skeleton Rusher:* Fast swarming melee attackers that charge past tanks to pressure backline heroes.
  - *Skeleton Archer:* Long-range snipers that fire arrows and retreat when approached.
  - *Necromancer:* Dark spellcasters that summon continuous waves of undead minions.
- **Themed Dungeon Rooms:** Distinct encounter chambers with varied enemy groupings, including Slime Pits, Bone Swarms, Archer Garrisons, and Necromancer Crypts.
- **Secret Hero (Torchero):** A mysterious torch-bearing hero capable of braving deep dark passages.

### Changed
- **Squad Grid & UI Polish:** Refined squad health status grid, streamlined damage meter readout, and updated retro typography.
- **Frontline Encounter Balance:** Rebalanced squad survival scaling against enemy swarm pressure.

---

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added
- **Original Soundtrack:** Added atmospheric background music across title screens, menus, and dungeon gameplay.

### Changed
- **Unified Squad Movement:** Removed individual hero speed stats so companions march together cohesively at the party leader's pace.
- **Combat Feel & Melee Rework:** Improved swing hit registration, attack reach, and recovery windows for all melee units.
- **Priest Tuning:** Faster Holy Smite cooldown and longer Divine Heal intervals to encourage active combat participation.
- **Tactical Map Overlay:** Smoother minimap scaling and improved visibility of explored vs. unexplored dungeon areas.
- **Performance & Stability:** Monster spawning and entity pooling optimizations for smooth frame rates during large swarms.

---

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added
- **Procedural Dungeon Exploration:** Randomly generated dungeon chambers and corridors with dynamic torchlight and destructible objects.
- **Fellowship Formation Movement:** Single-stick control scheme where recruited companions automatically navigate behind the leader in formation.
- **8 Playable Hero Classes:** Archer, Armored Axeman, Knight, Knight Templar, Priest, Soldier, Swordsman, and Wizard.
- **Survivor Recruitment & Leveling:** Rescue trapped companions to draft new party members and select class upgrade traits on level up.
- **Lava Slime Enemies:** Slime monsters featuring close-quarters attacks and ranged fire projectiles.
- **Tactical HUD & Retro Visuals:** Live party status, encounter damage meter, gamepad and keyboard (WASD) support, and toggleable CRT monitor filter.
