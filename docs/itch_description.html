Lead the Fellowship. Survive the Swarm. Escape the Dungeon.

Escapeteers is a top-down squad bullet heaven and horde survival game. Lead a frantic, auto-firing fellowship of survivors through an endless, dynamically generated dungeon. Act as the focal point of your party, steering your surrounding companions past deadly boss traps, smashing relentless monster nests, and rescuing caged recruits to replace fallen allies and grow your squad's firepower.

Core Features

  • The Fellowship Formation: Your squad surrounds you in layered rings rather than a single-file tail. When you stand still, companions form a protective circle around you; when you walk, allies ahead part out of your way and the group flows around your flanks and rear in a trailing crescent.
  • Stick-Only Spatial Control: Zero button micro-management. All shooting, melee strikes, and healing happen automatically. Gameplay revolves entirely around spatial positioning: stepping close enough for melee units to reach targets, and moving early enough to pull your trailing companions out of hazard zones.
  • Attrition & Recruitment: Rescue caged recruits scattered throughout the dungeon to replace fallen comrades and expand your party's firepower.
  • Endless Procedural Dungeon: Narrow corridors, chokepoints, and open arenas dynamically stream ahead as you explore.

The Squad Archetypes

  • Melee (Knight, Armored Axeman, Swordsman): Guard a leash range around their slot, stepping forward to strike and returning to the formation.
  • Ranged & Casters (Archer, Wizard): Anchor securely inside the formation to pepper enemies with arrows and spells from a distance.
  • Hybrid (Soldier): Shoots distant targets with a bow and draws a sword when enemies breach the inner ring.
  • Support & Hybrid Support (Priest, Knight Templar): Mend wounded allies with healing spells and restorative protective wards.

Controls

  • Move: Keyboard (WASD / Arrow Keys) or Gamepad (Left Stick / D-Pad)
  • Pause / Menu: Keyboard (ESC) or Gamepad (Start / Options)
