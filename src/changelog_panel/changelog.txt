[color=#ffffff][b]v0.4.0 - WIP[/b][/color]

[color=#e0e0e0][b]Added[/b][/color]
- [color=#ffffff][b]Auto-Sprint:[/b][/color] The squad picks up the pace automatically when no monsters are around.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Spike Traps:[/b][/color] Watch your step! Deadly floor spikes now pop up in rooms and hallways—take wide turns or your trailing buddies will pay the price.

[color=#e0e0e0][b]Changed[/b][/color]
- [color=#ffffff][b]Better Level-Up Scaling:[/b][/color] Heroes hit harder, survive longer, and attack faster as they level up.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Fragile Undead Swarms:[/b][/color] Summoned skeletons are now fragile fodder that go down in a couple of hits, dealing half damage with no heavy swings.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Necromancer Encounter Pacing:[/b][/color] Necromancers are tougher to withstand combat while their minions swarm, appearing solo on early floors before pairs emerge deeper in crypts.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Deeper Dungeon Pacing:[/b][/color] Early rooms warm you up gently before the deeper floors throw giant hordes at you.

[color=#ffffff][b]v0.3.0 - 2026-11-11[/b][/color]

[color=#e0e0e0][b]Added[/b][/color]
- [color=#ffffff][b]Pick Your Leader:[/b][/color] Choose which hero leads the charge before entering the dungeon.
- [color=#ffffff][b]New Undead Threats:[/b][/color]
  • [b]Skeleton Rusher:[/b] Fast boneheads that sprint past your tanks to harass the backline.
  • [b]Skeleton Archer:[/b] Pesky snipers that shoot and run away when you get close.
  • [b]Necromancer:[/b] Creepy summoners that keep raising more skeletons until you take them down.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Themed Dungeon Rooms:[/b][/color] Rooms now have their own flavor—Slime Pits, Bone Swarms, Archer Garrisons, and Necromancer Crypts.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Secret Hero (Torchero):[/b][/color] A mysterious torch-bearing survivor hiding in the dark for clever explorers to find.

[color=#e0e0e0][b]Changed[/b][/color]
- [color=#ffffff][b]Cleaner HUD:[/b][/color] Tidied up the squad health grid, damage meter, and pixel fonts.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Tougher Frontlines:[/b][/color] Tuned hero health and damage so your melee wall doesn't melt instantly.

[color=#ffffff][b]v0.2.0 - 2026-11-04[/b][/color]

[color=#e0e0e0][b]Added[/b][/color]
- [color=#ffffff][b]Original Soundtrack:[/b][/color] Retro dungeon tunes for the menus and battles.

[color=#e0e0e0][b]Changed[/b][/color]
- [color=#ffffff][b]Nobody Left Behind:[/b][/color] The whole squad now marches at the same speed so stragglers don't get lost.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Snappier Melee Combat:[/b][/color] Sword and axe swings connect more reliably and feel punchier.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Feistier Priests:[/b][/color] Priests spend less time over-healing and more time smiting monsters.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Clearer Minimap:[/b][/color] Easier to see where you've been and which rooms are still unexplored.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Smoother Swarms:[/b][/color] Big monster waves run buttery smooth without frame drops.

[color=#ffffff][b]v0.1.0 - 2026-11-04[/b][/color]

[color=#e0e0e0][b]Added[/b][/color]
- [color=#ffffff][b]Procedural Dungeons:[/b][/color] Endless torch-lit rooms and corridors to explore.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Single-Stick Squad Control:[/b][/color] You steer the leader; your fellowship follows in formation and fights automatically.
- [color=#ffffff][b]8 Hero Classes:[/b][/color] Archer, Armored Axeman, Knight, Knight Templar, Priest, Soldier, Swordsman, and Wizard.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Rescue & Level Up:[/b][/color] Bump into lost survivors to draft new heroes and upgrade your classes with XP.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Lava Slimes:[/b][/color] Grumpy fire-spitting blobs guarding the dungeon.
- [color=#ffffff][b]Retro Arcade Vibes:[/b][/color] Squad HUD, live damage meter, controller + WASD support, and an optional CRT filter.
